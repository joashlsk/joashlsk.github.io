Optimization Project

Objective: Balance economical and physical realities to find the absolute cheapest generation schedule that meets demand. We use linear programming to solve for this optimum.

Optimization

Problem Statement

DC-Optimal Power Flow finds the cheapest generation schedule respecting physical transmission limits. We extend it with four Singapore sustainability constraints.

Technical Approach

We modeled Singapore's transmission network as a graph of generator and demand buses (Jurong East, Clementi, Woodlands, Tampines, Bedok) connected by transmission lines, then formulated the dispatch problem as a linear program in Julia using the JuMP optimization framework.

The model uses the DC power flow approximation, where real power flow on each line is a linear function of the voltage-angle difference between its end buses divided by line reactance. This linearization keeps the entire problem an LP, allowing us to solve to a global optimum efficiently. The core formulation includes:

  • Objective: minimize total generation cost, the sum of each generator's marginal cost multiplied by its output.
  • Nodal power balance: at every bus, generation minus demand equals the net power flowing out across connected lines (Kirchhoff's law).
  • Transmission & generation limits: line flows stay within thermal capacity and each generator dispatches within its minimum/maximum bounds.
  • Reference bus: one bus's voltage angle is fixed at zero to anchor the angle solution.
  • Linearized losses: resistive line losses approximated linearly and folded into the balance equations.

On top of the base DC-OPF we layered four Singapore sustainability constraints: a carbon-emissions cap, a minimum renewable-energy share, and a spinning power-reserve margin — extending the classic least-cost dispatch into a sustainability-aware one. Solving the extended LP in Julia produced the optimal flow on every line and generation level at every bus, yielding a total cost of $531,317 while meeting full demand and every physical and sustainability constraint.
